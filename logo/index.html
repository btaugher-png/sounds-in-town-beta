Usage rules

✓ Do
  • Use the wordmark on dark or light backgrounds only
  • Keep the gold accent for key UI moments and the logo
  • Use Playfair Display for all headlines and display text
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "S" around the logo
  • Use the icon version for small sizes (under 120px wide)
✕ Don't
  • Don't place the logo on busy or patterned backgrounds
  • Don't change the typeface or letter spacing of the wordmark
  • Don't use gold text on a gold background
  • Don't stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
  • Don't use Ember (orange-red) as a primary brand color
